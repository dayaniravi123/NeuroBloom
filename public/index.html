Responsible AI · runs entirely on your device

Recovery companion

Ask about concussion recovery, screens, sleep, headaches, exercise, or how you're feeling. A machine-learning classifier runs in your browser — nothing you type is ever sent anywhere. Every message is screened for danger signs and distress first.

The companion is an on-device machine-learning model (a Naive Bayes classifier trained in your browser). It offers general education, not medical advice or diagnosis, and cannot see or treat you. For worsening or danger symptoms, or any emergency, contact local emergency services. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988.

Your space, your data

Settings

Everything you enter is stored only in this browser (local-first). Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data will erase it.

How the companion works

The recovery companion is 100% on-device: a small machine-learning model (multinomial Naive Bayes) is trained in your browser when the app loads, and classifies each question into a recovery topic. A separate deterministic safety net checks every message for danger signs and distress before the model, so those are never left to a probability.

No API keys, no servers, no third-party services. Nothing you type or track ever leaves this device.
